# Echo: the numbers we may say

Every sentence below quotes a row of [PROOF.md](../PROOF.md), sections "Replayed from recordings taken on the kit" and "Live on the kit". Say them word for word, on stage, on screen and on Devpost. A number that is not here is not said.

What stands behind all of them: one person, one night, replayed through the brain from recordings taken on the kit. The recordings were split into a tuning set and a held-out set before the settings were chosen, and these numbers come from the held-out set only.

## Say these

1. "One person stood on the spot for ten seconds, ten times, and Echo found them 10 of 10 times; with this few trials the true rate is probably between 69% and 100%."
2. "Five of those ten stands were with the second device hidden in a backpack, and it found 5 of 5; with this few trials the true rate is probably between 47% and 100%."
3. "With nobody on the spot it said somebody 0 times in 9.4 empty minutes; with this little time the true rate is probably under 0.4 a minute."
4. "Once the signal dropped, the decision came 0.50 to 0.58 seconds later; that leaves out the time a person takes to step in, which we did not record."
5. "In one session of standing still, 167 of 170 decisions said still, and in one session of stepping about, 154 of 170 said moving: one session each, so it shows it can work, not how often. Every miss was in the first seconds: the word takes about 3 to 5 seconds to change."
6. "With the second device low on a chair seat it found 0 of 5 stands. That is a limit: the device has to be at about desk height."
7. "Only the Wi-Fi line decides: in the open a person took 11 to 24 dB off it and the empty line dipped 2 dB at most, while the Bluetooth lines dipped 7 to 14.5 dB with nobody there."
8. "Those numbers are one spot, one person and one night, replayed from recordings taken on the kit. In one run with the board deciding by itself at the time, it said an arrival for 10 of 10 stands and none in the two empty minutes after."

## Do not say

- A bare percentage ("100% detection"). Say the count and its range.
- Any number for walk-throughs, someone crossing a metre to the side, standing behind the device, or a thing left in the line. Each was seen once and used for tuning: it is not a result.
- "Live" for sentences 1 to 7: they are replayed. The one live run is sentence 8, and it is a count of the arrivals the board said: it gives no delay, no still / moving number and no "nothing false", because the board's moment-by-moment decisions were not recorded in that run.
- Anything measured on a laptop about the sealed record as if it were measured on the board. Those numbers belong in the docs with "on a laptop" beside them, and not in PROOF.md.
- Anything simulated.
