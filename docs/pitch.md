# Radio Sight: Spoken Pitch

_Start with the room display and hardware visible._

> This is Radio Sight: Wi-Fi that understands the room. It maps how people and objects change radio links across a space.

_Point to the board, then the Pi._

> Everything runs locally on the Arduino UNO Q's Dragonwing CPU. The Pi is only the router and screen. A camera labeled zones during setup. The camera is now unplugged, no image is saved, and live sensing uses radio alone.

_Invite the judge into the marked square._

> As our judge walks in, the radio X-ray changes on the floor. Its moving layer follows fresh activity; its static layer shows persistent change since baseline. The screen reports a person, motion, and a zone.

_Ask the judge to make the practiced wave without changing zones._

> This wave creates a check-in: a deliberate radio pattern recognized without a phone or button.

_Place the metal tray at its marked spot._

> Now we place the metal tray by the router. Radio Sight separates its stable object pattern from the person and explains the changed link. The advisor recommends a tested router position. Before the move, the link measures __ dBm.

_Move the router to the recommended floor mark and wait for the result._

> After the move, the same link measures __ dBm. Dead-zone improvement is __. That is measured, not estimated.

_Finish on the proof strip while the unplugged camera cable remains visible._

> The proof strip shows zone accuracy __, confidence __, person-versus-object accuracy __, decision latency __, and bytes sent outside our local network __. Radio Sight delivers private, room-aware feedback: dead zones, presence, movement, check-ins, and advice, without the cloud.
