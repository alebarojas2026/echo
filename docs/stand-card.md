# Echo: judging desk card

1. Place the Arduino UNO Q board and Pi A on one desk, and Pi B on the row behind at desk height.
2. Tape Pi B and its power cable in place.
3. Power up the board, both Pis and the screens.
4. Set the board's and both Pis' clocks after power-up, before the baseline; Pi B's clock resets whenever it loses power.
5. Check the empty line reads about -44 dBm or better and a person on the spot takes 12 dB or more off it; nudge Pi B until it does, then secure the tape again.
6. Take the baseline with the spot empty and wait for it to finish.
7. Press `P` on the judge's page, click the desk the board and Pi A are on, then press `Enter`.
8. Each screen laptop joins the kit's own Wi-Fi and opens its page: judge's page `http://192.168.77.2:8080/`, engine room `http://192.168.77.2:8080/engine/`; the phone joins it too and opens the alarm page `http://192.168.77.2:8080/phone/`.
9. Invite the judge to step onto the spot and stand; wait for the figure to appear.
10. Have the judge stand still until the no-movement alert, then step aside and back; wait for the check-in confirmation.
11. Have the judge step out, arm while the spot is empty, then have the judge step in and stay until the intrusion appears.
12. Disarm and ask "What happened?"; show the answer, its steps and the records it used.
13. Press Prove it on the second screen; show the altered record named in the copy and the real record still verified.
14. Describe the silent witness in one sentence, without performing it: "If a witness goes silent, the record keeps counting and notes the gap."

A bag that blocks the line as much as a body reads as a person standing still.

**never during judging:** unplug Pi B, move any device, copy files over the kit's Wi-Fi.
