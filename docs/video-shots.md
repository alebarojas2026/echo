# Echo: Three-minute Video Shot List

A witness that never watches. It can't see you, it can't be edited, and it still tells you exactly what happened. The laptop shows the local interface; the phone is another local screen.

Use the one-spot kit: Arduino UNO Q and Pi A on the desk, Pi B across the gap at desk height. This is a planned edit, not measured timing. No face that can be identified. No number that is not in PROOF.md may be presented as a result, and the only numbers shown are the sentences in numbers-to-say.md. Capture only the live desk, never a simulated scene. Keep all media outside the repository.

## 0:00–0:30 — Found you

**Source:** Desk view of the three labelled devices and Screen capture of the main laptop.
**Picture:** Start on the problem: a room that needs a record and cannot have anything watching it. A person steps onto the spot and stands still; the figure appears, the no-movement alert comes, and they step aside and back for the check-in.
**Voiceover:** Stand between two small computers and your body weakens the Wi-Fi signal between them. Found you: the Arduino UNO Q decides on device, on its Qualcomm Dragonwing CPU.
**On screen:** `Found you` · `Live` · `No camera, no microphone`.

## 0:30–1:00 — Intrusion alert

**Source:** Desk view including the real phone and Screen capture from the same run.
**Picture:** The person steps out, the Operator arms, the person steps back in and stays. Hold for the actual intrusion and the alarm page turning red; no invented notification.
**Voiceover:** Armed means this spot should be empty. Step in and stay, and the board raises the alert on the alarm page.
**On screen:** `Armed`, then `Intrusion`.

## 1:00–1:25 — Tamper alert

**Source:** Desk view of the unplug and Screen capture of the alert and the engine room.
**Picture:** Keep Pi B's cable and the screen in one continuous view while unplugging. Show the alert naming the device and that witness going silent while the record keeps counting, then plug it back in.
**Voiceover:** Unplug one of its own devices and Echo says which one went quiet. The gap goes into the record.
**On screen:** `Tamper alert` · `Witness silent`.

## 1:25–2:00 — What happened

**Source:** Screen capture of the live answer and the records it cites.
**Picture:** Tap "What happened?". Show the answer with clock times, then its steps and the cited records.
**Voiceover:** Everything it senses is a record, and each one carries the fingerprint of the one before. Ask what happened, and it answers from that sealed record only.
**On screen:** `What happened?` · `From the sealed record`.

## 2:00–2:30 — Engine room

**Source:** Screen capture of the engine room on the second laptop.
**Picture:** The three devices, the record counting up and the signatures arriving. Press Prove it and hold on the line naming the altered record.
**Voiceover:** Two other machines sign the record as it grows. Prove it changes one record in a copy, and the board names it; the real record is never touched.
**On screen:** `Engine room` · `Prove it`.

## 2:30–3:00 — Proof

**Source:** Screen capture of the measured sentences, with a closing Desk view.
**Picture:** Show the sentences from numbers-to-say.md as full-screen text cards, word for word with their ranges, then finish on the UNO Q and the two Pis.
**Voiceover:** One person, one night, replayed from recordings taken on this kit; the counts and their ranges are on screen. We dug into a signal every building already has. It can't see you, it can't be edited, and it still tells you exactly what happened.
**On screen:** `Echo` · `MadHacks` · `Qualcomm / N+1`.

## Editor's notes

The text cards in the last shot are copied from numbers-to-say.md and checked against it before export: the count and its range, never a bare percentage, and "replayed from recordings taken on the kit" for every sentence except the one live run. Results that are not measured stay off the video: `Bytes sent outside local network: __`, `Images stored: __`.

If shortening the edit, cut the Tamper alert first, then the Engine room. Keep Found you and at least one of Intrusion alert or What happened; never edit away a failed check to imply success.
